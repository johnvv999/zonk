How Zonk works

Roll six dice. Set aside at least one scoring die, then choose: bank what you've got, or roll what's left and risk it all.

Your turn

  • Every roll, you must keep at least one scoring die.
  • Dice that don't score can't be kept — the app won't let you.
  • Bank once you have 300 in the turn, or 500 if you haven't scored yet this game. Setting aside four or five dice also unlocks banking.
  • If a roll shows nothing that scores, you zonk: the turn ends and the turn's points are gone.
  • If all six dice end up set aside, they come back hot and you roll again.

Winning

Reach the target and everyone else gets one last turn. Highest total takes it.

The odds of zonking

  • Six dice — about 2%
  • Three dice — about 28%
  • Two dice — about 44%
  • One die — about 66%
