How Zonk works

Roll six dice. Set aside at least one scoring die, then choose: bank what you've got, or roll what's left and risk it all. First to 10,000 triggers the final round.

Three is the whole set. A fourth matching die adds nothing on its own - unless it's a 1 or a 5, which always score singly. So four 2s is still 200, and the odd one can't be kept.

Your turn

  • Every roll, keep at least one scoring die. Dice that don't score can't be kept.
  • Bank once you have 300 in the turn, or 500 if you haven't scored yet this game.
  • A roll with nothing scoring is a zonk: turn over, turn points gone.
  • All six dice set aside is hot dice - they come back and you have to roll again. No banking on six for six.

Winning

Reach 10,000 and everyone else gets one last turn. Highest total wins, and writes a line for the hall of fame.

Odds of zonking

  • Six dice - about 2%
  • Three dice - about 28%
  • Two dice - about 44%
  • One die - about 66%
